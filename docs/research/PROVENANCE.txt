APPLE APP CLIP SIZE
https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-uploads/maximum-build-file-sizes/
As currently documented: physical App Clip Code / QR / NFC invocation must fit 15 MB for iOS 16+; before iOS 16 the cap is 10 MB. The current 100 MB allowance requires iOS 17+, digital-only invocation and reliable-network usage. Measure the uncompressed variant after app thinning, not the compressed download or simulator build. Keep a physical restaurant QR clip below 15 MB, preferably with margin. Remote dish photos and system fonts reduce bundle weight.

APPLE LIVE ACTIVITIES
https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/offering-live-activities-with-your-app-clip
App Clips may embed a dedicated widget extension that contains Live Activities only. Set extension bundle ID with Clip bundle ID prefix and add App Clip Extension capability (com.apple.developer.on-demand-install-capable). A full-app widget extension cannot be reused as the same embedded extension in the Clip; use separate extension targets sharing source. ActivityKit supports Live Activity creation/updates; a timer-driven foreground demo is adequate for local presentation, but background progress in production needs push updates. App Clips cannot rely on ordinary background execution.
https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/choosing-the-right-functionality-for-your-app-clip

IPHONE TESTING
https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/testing-the-launch-experience-of-your-app-clip
Build/run the App Clip scheme on the physical signed iPhone to cache it. Xcode scheme Run arguments can supply _XCAppClipURL for invocation-context debugging. To test the full QR card: iPhone Settings > Developer > Local Experiences > Register Local Experience; enter invocation URL, Clip bundle ID, title/subtitle/action and image, then scan a QR containing that URL. Local QR/NFC experiences do not require a deployed associated domain. TestFlight does require associated-domain setup; upload the containing full app and configure the Clip experience. App Clips do not get a normal Home Screen icon.
